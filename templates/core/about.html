About CyberShield AI

An innovative cybersecurity project built to protect people and businesses from modern digital threats.

🎯 Our Mission

CyberShield AI is designed to make cybersecurity easier to understand and use. We analyze suspicious URLs and messages, evaluate password strength, and provide security alerts with practical recommendations — helping you stay safe online.

🛠️ What We Do

  • 🔍 URL Scanning — Detect phishing and malicious links
  • 💬 Message Analysis — Spot fraud patterns in SMS and email
  • 🔑 Password Strength — Grade your password and suggest improvements
  • 📊 Security Alerts — Clear recommendations, no jargon

📚 Quick Guides

How to spot a phishing link

  • Check the domain carefully — paypa1.com ≠ paypal.com
  • Look for HTTPS — but remember, HTTPS alone doesn't mean safe
  • Beware of shortened links (bit.ly, tinyurl) — scan them first
  • Hover over links to see the real destination

What makes a strong password?

  • At least 12 characters
  • Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
  • Avoid dictionary words, names, or dates
  • Use a unique password for every account
  • Consider a password manager

If a scan says "Dangerous"

  • Do NOT click the link or download attachments
  • Do NOT reply or enter personal information
  • Report phishing emails to your email provider
  • If it came from work, notify your IT/security team
